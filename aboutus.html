What is BioGENIE® ?

BioGENIE® is additive biodegradable plastics technology. BioGENIE® is a revolutionary plastic additive that significantly increases the biodegradation rate of traditional plastics to solve the global problem of plastic pollution. BioGENIE® is built on innovation, environmental friendly and sustainability.

BioGENIE® Plastic Additive

Plastics have made our lives easier in so many ways and in our daily. For some applications we can "diet" not to use plastic products, but it is not solution to plastic pollution with reducing production. We can use our biodegradation technology in plastic manufacturing to allow plastic faster biodegrade in environment.

Traditional plastics biodegrade very slowly, resulting in hundred million tons of non biodegradable plastic waste that ends up in landfills each year worldwide.

Our BioGENIE® plastic additive product greatly improves the biodegradation rate of plastic products. BioGENIE® will initiate plastic to biodegrade through a series of chemical and biological processes aerobic and anaerobic biodegradation in a landfill and marine environment.
