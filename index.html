What is BioGENIE® ?

BioGENIE® is additive biodegradable plastics technology. BioGENIE® is a revolutionary plastic additive that significantly increases the biodegradation rate of traditional plastics to solve the global problem of plastic pollution. BioGENIE® is built on innovation, environmental friendly and sustainability.

How BioGENIE Work

BioGENIE Content Mix Additives

When exposed to UV sun light, thecarbonyl groups from polymer willform free radicals along the polymerchain. These free radicals can thenreact with surrounding oxygen toinitiate a chain scission reaction,leading to the breakdown of thepolymer chain.

Aerobic Biodegradation

The Additive initiate the degradation inthe presence of oxygen, moisture and soil bacteria. It will be attacked by micro-organisms which degrades the polymer chain to shorter segments ofpolymer chain. These chains are converted into functional group fragments biomass (humus) and formCO2 and water (H2O) as end product.

Anaerobic Biodegradation

The Additive initiate the degradationwithout presence oxygen. It will beattacked by microorganisms whichdegrades the polymer chain to shorter segments of polymer chain.These chains are converted into functional group fragments biomass and form CO2 and methane (CH4).
