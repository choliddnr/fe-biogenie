Credibility

BioGENIE® has been tested by independent lab using international standard ASTM and ISO test methods.

ASTM D6954 : Aerobic biodegradation and soil germination
Tier 1 (ASTM D5208) : Oxidative degradation, breakdown by sunlight, heat, and condensation (UV exposure) to see how quickly the plastic starts fragmenting.
Tier 2 (ASTM D5338) : Biodegradation, measures how effectively microbes consume the fragmented material by testing for CO2 evolution in aqueous or controlled composting conditions.
Tier 3 : Ecological effects (Ecotoxicity), assesses the environmental impact of the degraded plastic residues on soil, plants, or organisms.
ISO 19679 : Aerobic Biodegradation in seawater (Marine Environment)
ISO 17899:2022 : Biodegradability of plastics in Anaerobic condition
ISO 15985 : Anaerobic biodegradation method by analysis of released biogas
Microplastic Testing : No microplastics were found (by Intertek Wilton lab UK)
21 CFR US FDA 177.1520 : Compliance to be used in food-contact material (packaging, containers)
RoHS EN 71 Part 3 : Compliance heavy metal trace elements content
